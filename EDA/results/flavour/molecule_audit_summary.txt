FINAL MOLECULAR-SIMILARITY DECISION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary molecular similarity = IDF-weighted Jaccard.
Formula = sum(IDF of shared molecules) / sum(IDF of union molecules).
Common molecules contribute less than rare molecules.
Low-evidence profiles are retained but flagged; they should not be treated as equally reliable downstream.
Identical molecular profiles are explicitly flagged.
Name candidates are review-only and are never silently mapped.

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A high molecular similarity means the two FlavorDB entities share a large weighted molecular profile.
It does NOT by itself mean that one ingredient is a valid substitute for the other.
The final substitution framework should combine this feature with graph/contextual compatibility, carbon footprint, nutrition preservation, and hard constraints.
