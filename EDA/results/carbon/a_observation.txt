SustainableFoodDB Carbon EDA Observations

1. Dataset contains 7,753 records and 5 relevant columns.

2. No duplicate rows were found. All columns have complete data except Count, which has 1 missing value. and that is 
blueberries,BLUEBERRY,0.93803984,0.64,

3. CarbonFootprint is strongly right-skewed (skewness = 1.88).
The mean is 4.14 while the median is 1.80, 
indicating that a smaller number of ingredients have substantially higher carbon footprints.

4. Count is extremely right-skewed (skewness = 20.71).
The median occurrence count is 2, while the maximum is 69,096. A small number of ingredients occur much more frequently than the majority.

5. Similarity has a mean of 0.870 and median of 0.861,
with values concentrated approximately between 0.81 and 1.00.

6. There are 75 Similarity values marginally greater than 1.
These values are extremely close to 1 and appear to be numerical precision effects rather than substantially invalid values.
They should not be removed without further investigation.

7. High-frequency ingredients include onion, butter, garlic clove, water, olive oil and egg.

8. High carbon-footprint records are dominated by beef-related ingredients.
The maximum observed CarbonFootprint is 27.72, associated with BEEF BONE FREE, while many beef-related ingredients map to BEEF WITH BONE with a CarbonFootprint of 19.54.

9. Some ingredient-to-SustainableFoodDB mappings appear potentially unexpected, such as milk → CREAM, flour → MILLET FLOUR, cinnamon → VANILLA and cumin → QUORN.
These mappings should be investigated before being used in downstream optimization.

10. The carbon dataset shows substantial variation in environmental impact across ingredients, which is relevant to the project's planned ingredient-substitution and sustainability objective.

Clean: No duplicates, only one missing Count.
Skewed: Most ingredients have low carbon/count values, few have very high values.
Extreme values: Beef has very high carbon footprint compared with many other ingredients.
Mapping: Some ingredient substitutions/mappings need verification.
