FlavorDB EDA Observations

1. Dataset Structure
- FlavorDB contains 936 unique entities, 1,788 unique molecules, and 60,263 entity-molecule mappings.
- No duplicate entity IDs, molecule IDs, rows, or entity-molecule pairs were found.
- The dataset therefore has a clean entity-molecule mapping structure.

2. Entity Categories
- Entity categories are unevenly distributed.
- Fish is the largest category with 120 entities, followed by Fruit (81), Dish (77), Herb (51), and Dairy (48).
- Several categories contain only a small number of entities.
- This category imbalance should be considered when performing category-wise analysis.

3. Molecules per Entity
- The average number of molecules per entity is 64.38, while the median is 46.5.
- The number of associated molecules ranges from 1 to 391.
- The large difference between the minimum and maximum indicates substantial variation in molecular coverage across entities.
- The distribution is right-skewed, with some entities having substantially more molecular associations than others.

4. Molecular Weight
- Molecular weight has a mean of 168.82 and a median of 154.19.
- Most molecules have molecular weights concentrated approximately between 100 and 250.
- The distribution is strongly right-skewed with skewness of 2.75.
- Several high molecular-weight values appear as outliers, with a maximum of 985.13.
- These extreme values should be investigated rather than automatically removed.

5. XlogP
- XlogP has a mean of 2.56 and a median of 2.40.
- Most values are concentrated around 0 to 5, with some negative and high positive values.
- The distribution is right-skewed with skewness of 1.49.
- There are 18 missing xlogP values out of 1,788 molecules.
- High xlogP values appear as outliers, with a maximum of 21.30.

6. Heavy Atom Count
- Heavy atom count has a mean of 11.84 and a median of 11.
- Most molecules contain approximately 6 to 20 heavy atoms.
- The distribution is strongly right-skewed with skewness of 2.51.
- Several high values appear as outliers, with a maximum of 69 heavy atoms.

7. Overall Observation
- FlavorDB has a clean entity-molecule mapping but shows substantial variation in molecular coverage and molecular properties.
- Molecular weight, xlogp, and heavy atom count are all right-skewed and contain high-value outliers.
- These distributions should be considered in later flavor similarity and molecular-level analyses.
- No major data-cleaning issue was identified from this basic EDA.
