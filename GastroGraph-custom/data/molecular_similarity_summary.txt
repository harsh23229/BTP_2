FLAVORDB MOLECULAR SIMILARITY
======================================================================

Primary molecular similarity metric:
IDF-weighted Jaccard similarity

Formula:
sum(IDF(shared molecules)) / sum(IDF(union molecules))

This is a molecular-profile similarity feature.
It is NOT a standalone substitution score.

INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
IDF weighting reduces the influence of molecules that occur in many FlavorDB entities.
Low-evidence profiles are retained and flagged rather than silently discarded.
Identical molecular profiles are retained and flagged.
A molecular similarity of 1 does not imply that two ingredients are valid substitutes.

The later substitution model should combine this feature with graph/context compatibility, carbon impact, nutrition preservation, and constraints.
